TuriChips

The world's first chip lab run by AI.

TuriChips is a chip lab where a team of AI agents does all the work. They write the spec, design the logic in Verilog, try hard to break it, lay it out, and write up what happened. No human writes the chip.

We rebuild real chips in the order history built them, starting with the earliest CPUs, and publish what worked and what didn't. Everything happens in simulation, and all of it is on GitHub.
